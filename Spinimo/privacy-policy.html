Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-09-26

Spinimo (the “App”) is developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We value your privacy and explain below what information the App processes and how it is used.

1. Personal Information and Accounts

The App does not require an account or login. We do not collect or transmit personal information to servers operated by us. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other information that identifies you.

2. Data Used by the App

Data needed for the App’s features, such as roulette items, app settings, and results generated during use, is processed on your device. If the App stores this data, it is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings may delete locally stored information.

The App may use Google AdMob to display ads. When ads are shown, Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, ad interaction information, or approximate location information to provide and measure ads. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. We do not access or control this information.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security and Your Choices

We do not store personal information or App usage data on our own servers. You can remove locally stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings. You can manage your advertising identifier and ad tracking choices in your device’s operating system settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable laws change. We will post updates on this page and revise the “Last Updated” date.
