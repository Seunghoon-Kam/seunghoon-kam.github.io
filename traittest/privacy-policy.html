Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2025-01-01

TraitTest - Big Five, MBTI ("the App") is developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam ("we", "us", or "our"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

We do NOT collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts or photos
  • Usage analytics
  • Device identifiers or IP addresses
  • Any personally identifiable information

No registration or login is required to use the App.

2. Data Used by the App

(1) Personality Test Data & Local Content

The App provides Big Five personality test and MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator) assessments. All test questions, scoring algorithms, and result interpretations are stored locally within the App bundle. No data is sent to any external server.

(2) Test Responses & Results

When you take personality tests, your responses and test results are stored only on your device using Apple's local storage system (UserDefaults). This includes your answers to test questions, calculated personality scores, and test result history. We cannot access or retrieve this data.

(3) Saved Test Results

If you save your test results or create profiles, this data is stored only on your device. All saved results and profiles remain on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

(4) Push Notifications (Optional)

If you enable notifications, the App uses the device's local notification system. Notification data is processed locally on your device and is not sent to any server.

(5) Advertising (AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google may collect device identifiers, ad interaction data, and approximate location information. This data is handled solely by Google and is not accessible to us.

For more information, please review Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Policy Overview

(6) In-App Purchase: Remove Ads (If Available)

The App may offer an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information.

3. Third-Party Services

The App includes Google AdMob for advertising. No other third-party services collect or process your data. We do not share any information with third parties beyond what is processed by Google.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security

Since no personal information is collected or stored, no personal data is transmitted or saved on servers. All data, including your test responses, results, and saved profiles, remains on your device. All personality test calculations and result generation are performed locally without any network transmission.

6. Your Rights

Because the App does not collect personal information, users do not need to request data deletion or modification. Deleting the App removes all local data stored on the device, including test results, saved profiles, and settings. You can also delete individual test results or profiles within the App at any time.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
