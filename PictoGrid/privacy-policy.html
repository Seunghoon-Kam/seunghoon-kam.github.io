Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-07-20

PictoGrid (“the App”) is a Nonogram puzzle game developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.

We do NOT collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or files from your device
  • Account registration or login credentials
  • Any personally identifiable information through our own systems

No registration or login is required to use the App.

2. Data Used by the App

(1) Puzzle Progress & Local Storage

The App stores game-related data only on your device using a local database (SQLite), including: puzzle progress, stage clearance status, statistics, coins, daily rewards, settings (such as language, music, vibration), custom puzzles you create, and Premium purchase status. Puzzle content bundled with the App is stored locally. This data is not sent to any server operated by us.

A local backup snapshot may be supported on your device. Cloud backup, if added in a future update, will be described in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

(2) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to earn hints, continue a puzzle, or claim free rewards)

Google may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, ad interaction data, and approximate location information in accordance with its own privacy policies. This data is processed by Google and is not accessible to us.

For more information, please review Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Policy Overview

(3) App Tracking Transparency (iOS Only)

On iOS, the App may request your permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising. You can allow or deny this request. If you deny tracking, the App still works; ads may be less personalized. This choice is managed by Apple’s system settings and is not stored on our servers.

(4) In-App Purchase: Premium

The App offers an optional Premium in-app purchase. Premium may remove banner and interstitial ads and provide daily benefits such as free hints and continues. Optional rewarded ads may still be available when you choose to watch them.

Purchases are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or banking information. Purchase and restore status is stored locally on your device and verified through the store platform. You can use “Restore purchases” on a new device signed in with the same store account.

(5) Sharing & App Updates

If you use the share feature, content is shared through your device’s system share sheet. We do not receive the shared content. The App may check for available updates through the app store platforms; any related network requests are handled by those platforms or standard update libraries, not by our own servers collecting personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising. We do not operate our own analytics or cloud backend for user data. We do not share information with third parties beyond what is processed by Google and the app store platforms (Apple, Google) when you make or restore a purchase, or when the App checks for updates.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security

Puzzle progress, settings, statistics, and Premium status remain on your device (or are verified through the app store). We do not maintain a central database of your personal or gameplay data.

6. Your Rights

You can delete locally stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing app data in your device settings. On iOS, you can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security. Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, there is no account-based data to export or delete from us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when we add features such as cloud backup. When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
