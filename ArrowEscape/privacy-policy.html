Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-06

Arrow Escape (“the App”) is a casual arrow-path puzzle game developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the App and its advertising and payment services.

1. Accounts and Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not operate an account system or a server that collects your personal information or game progress.

The App itself does not request access to your device’s GPS or location services. However, when ads are requested, Google AdMob may receive your IP address and use it to estimate your general location. This is approximate location inferred from network information, not GPS location requested by the App.

2. Game Data and Device Storage

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device using the platform’s local preferences storage. This includes level progress, current level, coins and hints, daily reward and reminder settings, language preference, and the local status indicating whether ads have been removed. Puzzle content is bundled with the App. We do not send this game data to a server operated by us.

Daily reward reminders, when enabled, are scheduled as local notifications on your device. The App does not send notification content or schedules to our servers.

3. Advertising and Data Processed by Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to provide banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. The App requests ads only when the SDK’s consent status allows it. The AdMob SDK may automatically collect and share the following information with Google:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate the device’s approximate location.
  • Device or other identifiers: including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, and identifiers associated with signed-in device accounts where applicable.
  • App interactions: such as app launches, ad taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics: app or SDK performance information such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Google may use and share these data with advertising partners and other service providers involved in ad delivery for advertising and marketing, ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention and security. We do not receive your raw IP address, advertising identifiers, or GPS location through the App.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Google’s handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Advertising Choices

Where Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) determines that a consent message is required, the App presents the UMP consent form during startup and requests ads only when UMP allows it. The current App does not provide a separate in-App control to reopen the UMP privacy options form.

On iOS, the App may ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking; if permission is not granted, the App requests non-personalized ads. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or manage ad personalization in your device’s privacy or ads settings. These controls affect ad personalization and identifiers; they do not remove all ads from the App.

4. In-App Purchase: Remove Ads

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or banking details. The App stores the local purchase entitlement status on your device and checks purchase or restore results through the relevant store. Purchase transaction information is handled by the store under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Third-Party Services and Sharing

The third-party services used by the App are Google AdMob for advertising and Apple’s App Store or Google Play for purchases. AdMob data is sent to Google and may be shared by Google with advertising partners as described above. Purchase information is sent to the relevant store to process or restore a purchase. We do not operate a separate analytics, crash-reporting, or gameplay backend.

6. Children’s Privacy and Audience

The App is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Google Play target audience is set to ages 13 and older.

7. Storage and Retention

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are not stored in an account or on our server. Purchase records are handled by the app store and eligible purchases can be restored through the store. The App does not provide an account-based data deletion process. Retention periods for information processed by Google or the app stores are determined by those providers; please refer to their privacy policies for details.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.
