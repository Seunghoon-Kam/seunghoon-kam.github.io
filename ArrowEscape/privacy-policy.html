Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-06

Arrow Escape (“the App”) is a casual arrow-path puzzle game developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, how it is handled, and your rights.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.

We do NOT collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or files from your device
  • Account registration or login credentials
  • Any personally identifiable information through our own systems

No registration or login is required to use the App.

2. Data Used by the App

(1) Game Progress & Local Storage

The App stores game-related data only on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences), including: cleared levels, current level, in-progress puzzle sessions, coins, hints, daily reward streak, language preference, reminder settings, and whether ads have been removed via purchase. Puzzle content bundled with the App stays on the device. This data is not sent to any server operated by us.

(2) Local Notifications (Optional)

If you enable daily reward reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. Notification scheduling is processed locally on your device. No notification data is sent to our servers. You can turn reminders off in the App or in your device settings.

(3) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to earn coins or hints)

Purchasing Remove Ads hides banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available when you choose to watch them.

Google may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, ad interaction data, and approximate location information in accordance with its own privacy policies. This data is processed by Google and is not accessible to us.

For more information, please review Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Policy Overview

(4) App Tracking Transparency (iOS Only)

On iOS, the App may request your permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising. You can allow or deny this request. If you deny tracking, the App still works; ads may be less personalized. This choice is managed by Apple’s system settings and is not stored on our servers.

(5) In-App Purchase: Remove Ads

The App offers an optional one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase to remove banner and interstitial advertisements. Purchases are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or banking information. Purchase and restore status is stored locally on your device and verified through the store platform. You can restore purchases on a device signed in with the same store account.

(6) Sharing

If you share the App, content is shared through your device’s system share sheet. We do not receive the content you share.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising and the Apple App Store or Google Play Store to process purchases. We do not operate our own analytics or cloud backend for gameplay data. We do not share information with third parties beyond what is processed by Google and the app store platforms.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security

Progress, economy data, settings, and purchase status remain on your device (or are verified through the app store). We do not maintain a central database of your gameplay or personal data.

6. Your Rights

You can delete locally stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing app data in your device settings. You can disable local notifications in the App or in system settings. Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, there is no account-based data to export or delete from us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
