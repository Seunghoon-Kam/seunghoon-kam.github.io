Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-11-12

Pirate Roulette (“the App”) is developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the App, its advertising services, and app store billing services.

1. Accounts and Information We Collect

The App does not require an account or login. We do not operate an account system or a server that stores your personal information or gameplay data.

The App does not request access to your device’s GPS or location services. However, Google AdMob and advertising partners may receive your IP address when an ad request is made and may use it to estimate your general location. This is approximate location inferred from network information, not GPS location requested by the App.

2. Game Data and Local Storage

The App processes gameplay on your device. It stores game and preference data locally using the platform’s preferences storage, including remaining daily plays, whether you have received unlimited plays for the day, your language preference, sound and vibration settings, and whether you purchased Remove Ads. Advertising consent status, advertising personalization choice, and the iOS App Tracking Transparency status are also stored locally. This information is not sent to a server operated by us.

The App does not provide cloud synchronization for game progress. Locally stored game and preference data is managed by your device platform. The App has no account-based data deletion process because it does not maintain user accounts or store gameplay data on our servers.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

The App uses Google AdMob and includes mediation adapters for ironSource, Unity Ads, and Meta Audience Network. The App displays banner ads and optional rewarded video ads that you may choose to watch, for example, to receive more plays. Whether a mediation partner participates in a particular ad request depends on the ad request and the mediation configuration.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following information with Google for ad serving:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate the device’s approximate location.
  • Device or other identifiers: such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
  • App interactions: such as app launches, ad taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics: app or SDK performance information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Depending on the ad request and the mediation partner involved, these advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location for advertising and marketing, ad measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention or security. Google and participating ad partners may receive or share information as needed to provide and measure ads. We do not receive raw IP addresses, advertising IDs, or GPS location through the App.

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for Android is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Each advertising partner’s data practices and security measures are governed by its own privacy policy.

For more information, please review the providers’ privacy policies and disclosures:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

4. Advertising Choices

The App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent status and show a consent form when required by the configured privacy messages and your region. Ad requests are made only when the App’s consent checks allow them. The current App does not provide a separate in-App control to reopen UMP privacy options after the form has been presented.

On iOS, the App may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking. If tracking is not authorized, the App requests non-personalized ads when permitted by the applicable consent signals, and it tells Meta Audience Network that tracking is not authorized. You can change the ATT choice in iOS Settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or manage ad personalization through your device’s privacy or ads settings. These choices affect identifiers or ad personalization; they do not necessarily remove ads from the App.

5. In-App Purchase: Remove Ads

The App offers an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase to remove ads and unlock unlimited plays. Purchases and restores are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or banking information. The App stores the Remove Ads entitlement status locally and provides a Restore Purchases option in Settings. Purchase transaction information is handled by the relevant store under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Sharing

If you choose to share the App, the App Store link is passed to your device’s system share sheet. We do not receive the content of your share or information about the people with whom you share it.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through systems operated by us. Advertising SDKs may process information as described in this policy and their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security and Retention

We do not maintain a server or database containing your personal information or gameplay data. Game and preference data remains in local app storage on your device. Google’s stated encryption-in-transit practice for its Android Mobile Ads SDK is described above. Other advertising and app store providers handle data under their own security practices and retention policies; this App does not set those providers’ retention periods.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
