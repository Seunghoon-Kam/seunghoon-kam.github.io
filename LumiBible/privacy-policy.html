Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-18

Lumi Bible (“the App”) is developed and operated by Seunghoon Kam (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information by us.

We do NOT collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or files
  • Login/account credentials (no account required)
  • Any personally identifiable information

2. Data Used by the App

(1) Bible Content & Local Data

The App provides Bible reading and related features (e.g., bookmarks, reading progress, and settings). Any saved preferences or app data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a user account system, and we do not upload your content to our servers.

(2) Advertising (AdMob)

The App may use Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect device identifiers, ad performance data, and approximate location information for advertising purposes. This data is processed by Google and is not accessible to us.

For more information, please review Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Policy Overview

(3) In-App Purchase: Remove Ads (Optional)

The App may offer an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed securely through the platform store (Apple App Store and/or Google Play). We do not receive or store any payment information.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use Google AdMob for advertising. We do not share personal information with third parties other than what is processed by Google for ads.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Security

We do not collect personal information on our servers. Any app data you save (e.g., bookmarks/settings) remains on your device under the platform’s protections.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information, users do not need to request deletion, modification, or export of personal data from us. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored app data from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
